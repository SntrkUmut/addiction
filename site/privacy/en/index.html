In short

Quit works without an account. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We do not see, collect, sell or share it. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Information stored on your device

The following stays inside the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your daily average, pack price and pack size, currency, start date and optional personal reason
  • Your check-ins, the coping tools you used and for how long, any slips you chose to note, and their times
  • Milestones, lights and fox accessories you earned
  • Your appearance (System/Light/Dark), haptics and reminder preferences

This is used only to show your day counter, your approximate money estimate and your progress. Your device's own security (such as your passcode) protects it.

Internet connection

At most every few hours, the app connects to our server (Supabase, hosted in the European Union, Frankfurt) to download new content such as sanctuary chapters. This request contains no information about you; it fetches public content. As with any internet connection, the hosting provider may briefly keep technical logs such as IP addresses. We do not use them to identify or track you. [ONAYLA / CONFIRM: Supabase region and log retention]

Notifications

The app asks for notification permission only if you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain a generic message, never health or plan details. You can turn them off in the Ben (Settings) screen, and revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Export

Ben → "Verini dışa aktar" (Export your data) creates a JSON file of your data. You decide whether and where to share it, and that service's terms apply there.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes it. We hold no account or copy on your behalf. You can permanently delete all your data from this device in the app via Ben > Verilerimi sil.

Children

Quit is designed for ages 13+ and does not knowingly collect information from children (it collects no personal information from anyone).

Medical disclaimer

Quit is a self-management tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a healthcare professional about your health. In Türkiye you can call the ALO 171 quit line.

Changes

If optional features such as cloud backup are added, this policy will be updated before that app update, and the new date will be shown here.
